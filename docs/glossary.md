# Glossary

**Agency** — A participant's ability to make informed choices and act on them without coercion.

**Connection** — A relationship or channel established for a defined purpose, with agreed expectations and boundaries.

**Facilitator** — A person or organization that helps establish, support, or monitor a connection.

**Informed consent** — A voluntary decision made after receiving understandable information about purpose, risks, benefits, alternatives, and withdrawal.

**Minimum necessary** — The smallest amount of information, access, or duration needed to accomplish the stated purpose.

**Participant** — Any person directly involved in or affected by a connection.

**Safeguarding** — Actions taken to prevent and respond to abuse, neglect, exploitation, coercion, or other serious harm.

**Sensitive information** — Information whose exposure could create risk, stigma, discrimination, unwanted contact, or other harm.

**Withdrawal** — A participant's decision to pause or end involvement without retaliation.
