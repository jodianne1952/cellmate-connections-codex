# Contributing

Thank you for helping improve the Cellmate Connections Codex.

## Before contributing

- Do not include personal data, case details, private correspondence, credentials, or information that could identify a participant.
- Use anonymized, composite, or fictional examples.
- Check local legal, institutional, and accessibility requirements.
- Explain the practical problem your change addresses.

## Proposing changes

1. Open an issue describing the need, context, and possible risks.
2. Submit focused changes with clear headings and plain language.
3. Include accessibility and privacy considerations.
4. Ask for review from people with relevant lived experience or subject-matter expertise when appropriate.

## Writing guidelines

Use respectful, person-first language. Distinguish requirements from recommendations. Avoid promises of guaranteed safety or confidentiality. Keep protocols actionable and provide version dates and review dates.

## Review standard

Changes should improve clarity, safety, privacy, accessibility, or usefulness without exposing sensitive information. Maintainers may request revisions or decline material that creates avoidable risk.
